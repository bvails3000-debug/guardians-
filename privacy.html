Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

The short version

Guardians does not collect, transmit, sell or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores

To build your training plan, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • Your profile: optional first name, age, the standard you are scored on (male/female), and branch
  • Your goal job, target tests and schedule
  • Fitness test results you enter
  • Your training diary: effort, energy, sleep, soreness, mood, pain areas, illness and notes
  • Reminder preferences

This data is kept in the app's local storage on your phone or browser. The developer has no server and cannot see, access or recover it.

Network, accounts and tracking

  • There are no accounts and no sign-in.
  • There is no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • The app works fully offline and makes no network requests with your data.

Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device by the operating system. No push-notification service is used.

Backups and deleting your data

You can export a backup from Settings. The backup is a file (or copied text) that you control; the app never uploads it.

To delete everything, use Settings → Erase all data or uninstall the app.

Health information

Guardians is a training tool, not a medical device, and does not provide medical advice. Test scores are estimates; confirm official standards with your recruiter or unit. Consult a medical professional before starting a new exercise program and about any persistent pain.

Children

Guardians is intended for people aged 17 and older preparing for military fitness tests.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
